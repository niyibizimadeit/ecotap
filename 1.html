Why EcoTap is the Greenest Choice for Professionals

100% Recycled Materials

Our cards are manufactured from post-consumer recycled PVC (rPVC), diverting plastic waste from landfills and eliminating virgin plastic production entirely.

Carbon Negative Production

Every EcoTap card is produced using renewable energy sources. For every card sold, we plant one tree through our partnership with reforestation projects in Rwanda's Gishwati Forest.

Battery-Free Technology

The NTAG213 NFC chip is passive — it draws no power and requires no charging. One card lasts a lifetime of networking without any electronic waste.

Zero Re-Printing Waste

Change jobs, titles, or phone numbers? Update your digital profile instantly — no need to discard and reprint physical cards ever again.

Ethical Supply Chain

All manufacturing partners are audited for fair labor practices, and 5% of profits support environmental education programs in Rwandan schools.

Measurable Impact Dashboard

Track your personal environmental savings: trees preserved, water conserved, and CO₂ emissions avoided — visible right inside your EcoTap profile.
