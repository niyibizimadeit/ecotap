Our Story: Born from a Vision for a Paperless Africa

Project EcoTap was conceived at the intersection of environmental urgency and technological innovation. In 2024, Team Environment Rwanda and RDMC Ltd joined forces after witnessing the staggering waste generated by traditional business cards at Kigali's international trade summits. What began as a pilot program for 500 local entrepreneurs has now evolved into a nationwide movement, positioning Rwanda as the first paperless-networking economy in Africa.

Our name, EcoTap, embodies our dual promise: ecological responsibility and the simple tap that connects professionals. We are not just selling a product — we are building an infrastructure for sustainable digital identity across the continent.

100% Recycled Materials

Post-consumer recycled PVC (rPVC) — diverting plastic waste from landfills. Each card represents approximately 8 plastic bottles intercepted from waste streams.

Carbon Negative Production

Renewable energy manufacturing + one tree planted per card sold. Our Gishwati Forest partnership has already restored 5,000+ native trees.

Battery-Free Technology

Passive NFC chip — lasts a lifetime without e-waste. No charging, no batteries, no planned obsolescence.

Community First

5% of profits fund digital literacy programs for Rwandan youth and women-led MSMEs. We believe green tech must also be inclusive tech.

Municipal Waste Mitigation

Each EcoTap card replaces an average of 1,000 paper cards over its lifetime. With 20,000 cards deployed in our pilot, we've diverted over 2.5 tons of paper waste from landfills in Kigali alone.

Projected by 2027: 50 tons of waste diverted.

Green Tech Jobs

Localized assembly, printing, and software roles for Rwandan youth. Our facility employs 15 full-time technicians and engineers, with plans to scale to 50+ jobs by Phase 2. 60% of hires are young women from technical training programs.

Water Conservation

Paper manufacturing consumes 17,000 gallons of water per ton. By eliminating paper cards, EcoTap saves an estimated 850,000 gallons of water annually per 10,000 users — enough to supply 200 Rwandan households for a year.

Reforestation Commitment

Through our partnership with Rwandan Forestry Authority, we plant one native tree for every card sold. To date: 5,000+ trees planted in Gishwati-Mukura National Park, restoring critical habitat for endangered primates.

Measurable ESG Impact for Corporate Clients

Our Corporate SaaS includes an ESG reporting dashboard that automatically calculates your team's environmental savings. Use it for sustainability reports, investor communications, and B Corp certification documentation.
